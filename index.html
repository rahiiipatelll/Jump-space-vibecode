Jump Space Power Grid Optimizer

Runs entirely in your browser. No server, no external API. Edit data/shapes.js whenever the game changes a shape.

1. Power grid

Click a cell to cycle it: blocked → open → shielded → blocked. Paint the grid to match whatever reactor/generator layout you've got in-game right now.

open shielded blocked

2. Components

Click a component to add one to your loadout. Click it again to add another copy.

Paint the shape (max 4×4 — the largest any known component uses), name it, and give it a section. It's saved on this device and will show up here every time you open this page, until you delete it.

3. Your loadout

Result

Component shapes are captured in data/shapes.js, based on the community tool at jump-space-grid-optimizer.vercel.app. The power grid itself is now painted by hand (see above) rather than assembled from preset reactor/generator shapes. See README.md for how to keep this current.
