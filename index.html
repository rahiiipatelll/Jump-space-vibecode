Jump Space Power Grid Optimizer

Runs entirely in your browser. No server, no external API. Edit data/shapes.js whenever the game changes a shape.

1. Reactor & Generators

Pick one reactor (4 rows) and up to two generators (2 rows each) to build the 8×8 grid. Order doesn't matter — reactors are always stacked above generators.
